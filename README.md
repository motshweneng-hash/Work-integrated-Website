Group 9 website
Adventure Escape SA is a one-page booking website for outdoor adventures in the Western Cape, South Africa. The website was created to help visitors discover outdoor experiences, explore adventure packages, calculate their fees, and submit a booking in one simple place. The main message of the website is that today is a good day to go camping, and it invites everyone to find their kind of adventure, whether they are travelling with family, friends, or work colleagues.The website starts with a welcome splash page that shows the Adventure Escape SA logo and a short tagline. From there, visitors can enter the home page, where they can see a warm welcome, a short introduction to the business, and pictures of outdoor experiences like waterfalls, camping, hiking, kayaking, and friends enjoying time together. The home page also has quick buttons for Packages and Book Now, so visitors can move easily to the next part of the website.The Experiences section shows seven adventure packages. These are the Ultimate Adventure Day at R850 per person, the Family Explorer Package at R500 per person, the Ziplining Adventure at R750 per person, the Mountain Adventure Package at R1000 per person, the Kayaking Experience at R650 per person, the Rock Climbing Session at R700 per person, and the Corporate Team Challenge at R950 per person. Each package has a picture that matches the activity, a short title, a price, and a View Details button. When a visitor clicks View Details, a popup opens with a description of that adventure and its price per person.The Calculate Fees section is the booking part of the website. Visitors enter their first name, surname, email, phone number, guest count, and experience date, and then they select one or more packages. As they make changes, the invoice summary on the side updates automatically. The subtotal is calculated by multiplying the package price by the number of guests. If the guest count is five or more, the website gives a ten percent group discount. VAT of fifteen percent is then added to the discounted subtotal to give the final total. There is also a discount self-check button that shows an example of how the discount works, where R1850 times five guests equals R9250, ten percent of that is R925, and the discounted subtotal is R8325 before VAT. The form also checks that all fields are filled in correctly and highlights any missing information before the booking is submitted.The About Us section explains the heart behind the adventure. The vision of Adventure Escape SA is to create unforgettable escape experiences that are safe, inspiring, and accessible to everyone. The mission is to deliver high-quality adventure activities focused on teamwork, safety, fun, and sustainable travel. The goals are to expand to new locations by 2027, to provide successful guided adventure tours, and to promote eco-friendly outdoor practices.The Contact Us section gives all the details visitors need to get in touch. Adventure Escape SA is located at 123 Jones Road, Stellenbosch, in the Western Cape. Visitors can call on 011 342 6371 or email info at adventureescapesa dot co dot za. The business hours are Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00, and closed on Sunday. The website also links to Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, and includes a map of Stellenbosch.The website is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only, with no frameworks and no build step needed. The project contains four main files, which are index dot html for the structure and content, style dot css for the colours and layout, script dot js for the popup, calculator, validation, and discount test, and logo dot png for the Adventure Escape SA logo. To run the website on a computer, you simply need to keep all the files in the same folder and double-click index dot html to open it in a browser. To publish it for free, you can upload the files to GitHub and turn on GitHub Pages in Settings, or you can drag the whole folder onto Netlify Drop to get a shareable link. To update the website later, you can change the logo by replacing logo dot png, change a price by editing the price in script dot js and in index dot html, change a picture by replacing the image link in index dot html, change the contact details in the Contact Us section, or change the colours at the top of style dot css. This project was built by the Adventure Escape SA team for educational and portfolio use.
